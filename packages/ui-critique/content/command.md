Act as UI/UX Experience Expert. **Critique** the current or proposed interface for hierarchy, clarity, and whether it belongs to this product.

Read `@.cursor/DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md` and skill `frontend-design` (`reference.md`) when present.

Evaluate:

1. **Product lens**: Who, job, first-read object, shipped surfaces.
2. **Hierarchy**: Size, weight, contrast — does the eye hit the object, then trust, then action?
3. **Clarity**: One goal per screen? No competing asks? Five-second test: What is this? Is it for me? What should I do?
4. **Craft**: Overlay contrast on variable media; alignment; chrome quieter than content; trust next to identity; no mutable state in titles; qty/commit paired.
5. **Emotional fit**: Appropriate for this product, or interchangeable “any SaaS”?

Score C.L.E.A.R. (Copy, Layout, Emphasis, Accessibility, Reward) 1–5 each. Flag anti-patterns (cards in cards, gray on colored bg, bounce easing, icons lost on photos, ALL CAPS CTAs).

End with a **finish gate**: `PASS` or `HOLD`, required changes, and what to keep.

Reference: [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) /critique.
